**Description** | Jalaster Silvermane is a respected captain of the Waterdeep City Watch and a member of the Lords' Alliance. Known for his integrity and sense of duty, Jalaster is deeply invested in the safety and future of Waterdeep. He is the current keeper of the legendary Luckblade, a family heirloom, and is willing to offer it as a reward for the city's protection.
