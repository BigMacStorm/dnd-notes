**Member of** | Proprietor of The Yawning Portal

**Description** | Durnan is the legendary proprietor of The Yawning Portal tavern in Waterdeep, known for his stoic demeanor and vast experience as a former adventurer. He oversees the entrance to Undermountain, offering advice, rumors, and warnings to those who dare descend. Durnan is respected by adventurers and feared by troublemakers.
