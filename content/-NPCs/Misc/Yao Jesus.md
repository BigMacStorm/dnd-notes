Yao Jesus is a doppelganger who once ran with [[The Undertakers]]. After being approached by the party and being restrained, he agreed to work as their assistant in return for his life. What began as potential servitude seems to have blossomed into a close if not friendly relationship. At the steady guidance of [[Gertie]], Yao is paid well, taken care of, and appreciated. 

> [!note] Editors note:
> 
   Yao Jesus, while an accepted member of the party, does not general engage in combat, conversation, or any physical endeavors. Think of him more as a friendly ghost.
