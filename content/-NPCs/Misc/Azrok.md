**Member of** | Legion of Azrok

**Description** | Azrok was the blind hobgoblin warleader of the Legion of Azrok, known for his tactical prowess and leadership. He wielded a magical dagger of truesight, which allowed him to lead his people. Azrok was embroiled in a conflict with House Auvryndar and ultimately sacrificed himself for his legion.

**Related Characters** | Lurkana (wife), [[T'rissa Auvryndar]] (rival)
