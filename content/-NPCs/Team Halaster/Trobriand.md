**Member of** | Halaster's Seven

**Description** | Trobriand is one of Halaster's Seven, known as the Metal Mage for his expertise in constructs, golems, and magical engineering. He is responsible for many of the mechanical dangers and wonders found in Undermountain, and serves as a teacher and enforcer for Halaster.

**Related Characters** | [[Halaster]], [[Nester]], [[Murial]], [[Arcturia]], The Party

**Involved in floors** | [[Floor 9 - The Dweomercore]], throughout Undermountain

# Major Events

* Created and maintained many of the constructs and golems in Undermountain.
* Served as a member of Halaster's Seven, teaching magical engineering and enforcing Halaster's will.
* His inventions and traps are encountered by adventurers throughout the dungeon.
