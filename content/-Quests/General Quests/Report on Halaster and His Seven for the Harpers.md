**Point of Contact** | [[Jorath Brighthelm]]

**Giver of Quest** | [[Jorath Brighthelm]] (on behalf of the Harpers)

**Reward** | Harper favor, information, and potential magical support

###### Details

Jorath Brighthelm, a journalist and covert agent for the Harpers, has tasked the party with uncovering more information about Halaster Blackcloak and his Seven. The Harpers are deeply concerned about the resurgence of ancient evil magicks in Undermountain and wish to keep them in check. Any knowledge the party can gather about Halaster, his lieutenants, and their activities should be passed along to Jorath, who will ensure it reaches the right hands within the Harpers.
