**Point of Contact** | [[Moonstar Family]]

**Giver of Quest** | The Moonstar Family

**Reward** | 1000 gold pieces each as a retainer, two fully funded missions, magical artifacts, and immense prestige and renown from the Moonstar Family

###### Details

The Moonstar Family, one of the oldest and wealthiest noble houses in the Sword Coast, has tasked the party with venturing deep into Undermountain to search for their friend, the bronze dragon Glyster. Glyster has been corrupted by disciples of Shar, and the family wishes for him to be freed if possible—or slain if there is no hope of redemption. The party has already received a generous retainer and is promised a mountain of rewards, magical artifacts, and the full support and prestige of the Moonstar Family for completing this dangerous mission.
