**Point of Contact** | [[Lady Rozner]]

**Giver of Quest** | Lady Rozner

**Reward** | Favor and gratitude of House Rozner, potential noble connections and rewards in Waterdeep

###### Details

Lady Rozner has asked the party to assist the Rozner family in recovering their broken name and restoring their reputation. Once a respected noble house in Waterdeep, the Rozners have fallen from grace due to scandal and political misfortune. By helping the family regain influence, mend alliances, and prove their worth, the party can help House Rozner rise once again as a respectable and powerful force in Waterdeep society.
