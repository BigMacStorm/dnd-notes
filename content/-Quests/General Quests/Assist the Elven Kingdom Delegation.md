**Point of Contact** | [[Elven Kingdom Delegation]], [[Lady Silverhand]]

**Giver of Quest** | Elven Kingdom Delegation

**Reward** | Magical gifts (such as the horn), elven favor, and potential recognition and alliances in Waterdeep

###### Details

The Elven Kingdom delegation has established a base in Waterdeep to investigate further artifacts now that the Coronal Throne has been returned. They seek to interact with the party further, hoping to gain recognition from the city and reclaim part of their ancestral home in Waterdeep. The elves have gifted the party a magical horn as a token of trust. They are working closely with Lady Silverhand, the most powerful and reclusive Lord of Waterdeep, to advance their cause and secure their place in the city's future.
