**Point of Contact** | [[Jalester Silvermane]]

**Giver of Quest** | Jalester Silvermane

**Reward** | The friendship of the Waterdeep Guard, renown with the Lord’s Alliance, and a Luckblade passed down through his family

###### Details

Jalester Silvermane, a captain of the watch of Waterdeep, has tasked the party with searching for a shard of a runestone that shattered thousands of years ago. The shard is said to possess great magical properties, and Jalester has stressed that recovering it is of utmost importance for the future of Waterdeep. He has not revealed the full reason for his need, but promises immense rewards: everything his family owns, the friendship of the Waterdeep Guard, recognition from the Lord’s Alliance, and a legendary Luckblade that has been in his family for generations.
