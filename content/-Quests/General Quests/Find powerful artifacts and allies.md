###### Details

Lord Marcus Kestellharp, an ambitious noble seeking to break into Waterdeep's ancient power structure, has hired the party to delve into Undermountain. His goal is to exploit the power and secrets within, helping rich families, meeting less reputable ones, collecting favors, and acquiring ancient artifacts to increase his influence. Marcus keeps his distance, using his trusted aid Todd to communicate with the party—usually inside the Yawning Portal.
