**Giver of Quest** | Sir Arthur Roland

**Reward** | Personal glory, magical power, and the legacy of becoming the greatest wizard knight

###### Details

Sir Arthur Roland is on a quest to become the greatest wizard knight of all time. His journey is filled with magical challenges, duels, and the pursuit of legendary artifacts and spells. While he is driven by ambition and honor, Sir Arthur also struggles with his unusual blue appearance—a topic he prefers not to discuss. This quest is about proving himself, overcoming magical and martial obstacles, and forging a legacy worthy of song and legend.
