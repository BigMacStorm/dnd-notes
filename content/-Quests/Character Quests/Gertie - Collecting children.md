**Giver of Quest** | Gertie

**Reward** | Personal fulfillment, gratitude from her homeland, and a positive reputation among certain halfling circles

###### Details

Due to a traumatic event in her past, Gertie feels compelled to find children in need and send them back to her homeland—a forest tribe of ghostwise halflings in the High Forest, north of the Star Mountains.

Gertie has enlisted the help of Samson and Delilah, wandering adventurers and friends of Durnan, to assist in escorting children. She has also made arrangements with Father Chris, the priest running the Waterdeep orphanage. After donating 500 gold, the orphanage has been renamed "Gertie's House," and Father Chris allows her to recruit children for her cause—so long as it does not attract the attention of the City Watch.

### Children recruited so far

Two children so far.

* A human child whose hand was lost in a giant scorpion accident.
* A small Kua-Toa child separated from her tribe after an adventuring party looted their artifacts.
