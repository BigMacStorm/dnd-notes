**Reward** | Personal satisfaction, a cool ship, and potential nautical adventures

###### Details

Cloud Stormbringer has set out on a personal quest to find a truly remarkable ship. Whether it's a magical vessel, a legendary pirate ship, or just something with a lot of character, Cloud is determined to row, sail, or commandeer the coolest boat in all of Waterdeep and Undermountain. This quest is all about the journey, the stories, and the ship itself.
