# Major Events

### Initial State

The Dungeon level was originally mostly occupied by The Undertakers, with The Xanathar Guild moving in on their territory. 

### What did you do there?

After being confronted by The Undertakers, and crushing their outposts, our party then ran into the Xanathar Guild, who kindly asked for their help in eliminating The Undertakers. This was quite an easy task since they were all already dead.
### Current State

The Xanathar Guild maintains a small outpost here now, but their attentions do seem to lie elsewhere.
