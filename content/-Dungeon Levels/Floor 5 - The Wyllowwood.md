# Major Events

### Initial State
Wyllow lived here in peace with her companions: her displacer beast pet, her butler Halastree, and her friend Tearulai, who was actually a magical sentient sword controlling the evil dragon Valdemar.

### What did you do there?
The adventuring party was asked by [[The Xanathar Guild]] to free Valdemar from Tearulai's control, but warned by Wyllow not to interfere. The party decided to upset everyone by killing Valdemar and freeing the sword Tearulai. Tearulai now resides with Jalaster Siolvermane, a captain of the city watch and member of [[The Lords Alliance]], as he missed being among the wealthy and elite.

### Current State
After apologizing, Wyllow decided to forgive the party, and they are now once again considered welcome in her home and forest.
