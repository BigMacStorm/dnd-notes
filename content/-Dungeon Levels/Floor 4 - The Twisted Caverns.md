# Major Events

### Initial State

The floor started with three main factions: the Drow of House Auvryndar, the Aboleth Illun, and the Kuo-Toa tribe. The Kuo-Toa were pushed out of their lake home by the Aboleth and sought the adventuring party's help, while the Drow waited patiently to see who would emerge victorious.

### What did you do there?

The adventuring party did not engage in diplomacy or investigation and decided to take a direct approach. They confronted the Aboleth and killed it, stealing all of the Kuo-Toa's ancient treasures before allowing them back into their home. Cloud Stormbringer tasked the Kuo-Toa with worshipping Thor before moving on.

### Current State

The Kuo-Toa have reclaimed their home and are now worshipping Thor as instructed by Cloud Stormbringer. Meanwhile, the Drow of House Auvryndar are eagerly watching and preparing to take advantage of the situation left in the party's wake.
