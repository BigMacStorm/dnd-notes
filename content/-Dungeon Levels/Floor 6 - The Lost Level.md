**Description** | Hidden deep within the Undermountain, this floor is a forgotten relic of dwarven history. The Underhalls of Melairbode, once the proud home of the Melairkyn clan led by King Melair, now lie in ruins, their tunnels collapsed by monstrous bugs. The walls are adorned with murals and carvings that tell the story of King Melair's rise to power and the formation of his clan, a haunting reminder of a bygone era.

**Notable Characters** | [[Skella Ironeye]]

# Major Events

### Initial State

The floor was a desolate and isolated place, its tunnels blocked and its treasures hidden beneath the rubble. Clan Ironeye, a group of opportunistic duergar raiders, stumbled upon this ancient dwarven stronghold and sought to plunder its artifacts. Their greed led them to disturb the peace of this forgotten place, but they were not prepared for what awaited them.

### What did you do there?

When the party arrived, they encountered Clan Ironeye and swiftly dealt with the raiders, leaving many of them dead. Their leader, Skella Ironeye, pleaded for her life and offered to help the party in exchange for mercy. Together, they uncovered the secret entrance to the throne room of King Melair. Inside, the party faced a harrowing battle against several giant demons guarding the ancient king's tomb. After a hard-fought victory, they looted the tomb, claiming the treasures of the Melairkyn clan.

### Current State

The floor now lies looted and abandoned, its secrets unearthed and its treasures taken. Only the remnants of gates and the collapsed tunnels remain, a silent testament to the glory and tragedy of the Melairkyn clan.
