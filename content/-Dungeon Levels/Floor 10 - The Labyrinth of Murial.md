# Major Events

### Initial State

House Auvryndar, led by the cunning Matriarch Vlone, held dominion over this floor. Their forces were locked in a deadly struggle with House Freth, vying for control of the Undermountain. Meanwhile, Murial prowled the labyrinth, her presence a constant threat to the Drow. The Merry Men, an adventuring party from your guild, had been captured and were forced to perform as musical entertainment for House Auvryndar.

### What did you do there?

The party's arrival was marked by a near-fatal encounter with Murial. Rescued by House Auvryndar, they were offered sanctuary by Vlone in exchange for dealing with Murial. The party accepted the task and, at Halaster's request, harassed and ultimately killed Murial. Their victory, however, was only the beginning.

A member of the Xanathar Guild approached the party with a new mission: to rescue Xaran A'daragon and his men, spies from House Freth who had been captured by House Auvryndar. The party infiltrated the Drow stronghold, freeing the spies and the Merry Men. In a dramatic turn of events, they assassinated Vlone and her adopted daughter Zress, leaving House Auvryndar in chaos.

### Current State

With Vlone and Zress dead, the power vacuum has left House Auvryndar in shambles. The Troglodyte general Gorzil has taken control of the floor, pledging his loyalty to the party. The Merry Men have been freed, and the labyrinth now stands as a shadow of its former self, its halls echoing with the remnants of a once-great conflict.
