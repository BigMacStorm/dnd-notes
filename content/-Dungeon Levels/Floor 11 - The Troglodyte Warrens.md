# Major Events

### Initial State

The floor was an active war zone, with remnants of House Auvryndar locked in a losing battle against House Freth. The Troglodytes, led by Koldann, maintained control of their territory while avoiding direct involvement in the Drow conflict. A giant semi-mechanical worm-like creature roamed the floor, indiscriminately killing anything in its path.

### What did you do there?

The party met with Koldann, who promised untold riches and magical items if they dealt with House Freth. While exploring, the party encountered remnants of House Auvryndar still battling members of House Freth. They reunited with their old ally Xaran A'daragon, who agreed to escort them to House Freth's headquarters.

On the way, the party came across a standoff between Drow warriors of House Freth and monstrous demons summoned by the late Lady Vlone. Seizing the opportunity, the party ambushed Xaran and his companions, killing them. They then convinced the demons to join their plan to eliminate House Freth. After a successful assault, the party left the floor devoid of Drow, leaving the Troglodytes as the ruling faction.

### Current State

With both Drow houses eradicated, the Troglodytes, under Koldann's leadership, now rule the floor. The mechanical worm continues to roam the tunnels, posing a threat to any who dare to enter.
