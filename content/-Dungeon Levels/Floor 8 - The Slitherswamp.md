# Major Events

### Initial State

The floor was controlled by the victorious Ssethian Scourges, a group of Spirit Nagas who had long ago defeated the Yuan-ti of this floor. They used a rod of rulership to enslave the denizens of the swamp, forcing them into perpetual servitude.

### What did you do there?

The party encountered the Spirit Nagas and a group of Bullywogs who had mysteriously teleported to this floor, led by their ruler Kuketh, an evil Slaad. With the help of Yutla, the Spirit Nagas were defeated, and the Bullywog Torbit was instilled as the leader of the floor. Kuketh agreed to step aside and stop tormenting the Bullywogs in exchange for the party's promise to find his control gem and grant him freedom. The party now possesses the gem and may need to fulfill their promise.

### Current State

The floor is now under the leadership of Torbit, a gullible but kind-hearted Bullywog ninja gentleman. Kuketh remains in the swamp, awaiting the return of his control gem.
