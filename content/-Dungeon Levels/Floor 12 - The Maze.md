It is home to Spiderwatch Keep, the stronghold of House Freth, a Drow family known for their cunning and ruthlessness. The keep is a fortress of black iron with silver veins, resembling a crouching spider.

# Major Events

### Initial State

The floor was under the control of House Freth, with their Matron Erallel Freth and her brother Drivvin overseeing operations. The keep was heavily guarded by Minotaurs, Vrocks, and Drow warriors. Guests were warned not to touch the massive webs or draw weapons, as doing so could cause disturbances.

### What did you do there?

The party initially gained entry to Spiderwatch Keep by tricking the Drow guards and claiming to have helped eliminate House Auvryndar. They were welcomed as guests by Erallel Freth, led by the friendly and happy to see them Xaran, who declared them friends of House Freth. However, tensions escalated when Chadwick presented half of the Matron of House Auvryndar to Erallel, using her refusal to accept it as justification to attack. This led to a massive battle within the keep. Malleen Shadowdusk, a guest of House Freth, observed the conflict without intervening. Xaran was killed in the conflict.

### Current State

The battle ended in disaster for the party, with Cloud being knocked unconscious due to poison, Chadwick and Azimuth dying, and the rest of the group being reduced to 0 HP. The fate of the floor remains uncertain, with House Freth likely consolidating their power after the skirmish.
