# Major Events

### Initial State

The floor is a functioning magical school, with Halaster and Wormriddle overseeing its operations. Students were engaged in magical studies and political maneuvering, with the necromancy teacher Nester being one of Halaster's Seven.

### What did you do there?

The party passed several of Halaster's tests, including the murder of several students, and were officially admitted to the Dweomercore. This granted them access to the school's library and resources. Along the way, they met several intriguing characters, including Spite Harrowdale, Skrianna Shadowdusk, the tiefling twins Violence and Turbulence, and Halaster himself.

### Current State

The party now has access to the Dweomercore's resources and library. The floor remains a hub of magical study and political intrigue, with the party's presence adding a new dynamic to the power struggles.
