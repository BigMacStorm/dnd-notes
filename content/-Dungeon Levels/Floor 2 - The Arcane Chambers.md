# Major Events

### Initial State

The goblin tribe was under the rule of Yek, who had transformed himself into a human using a magical crown. The Xanathar Guild maintained a presence here, but tensions were high due to the Drow and his wererat gang stealing a stone key from their base.

### What did you do there?

The party confronted Yek and removed him from power at the request of the goblins. They also encountered the Drow and his wererats, who handed over the stolen stone key after learning of the party's victory over the Xanathar Guild outposts.

### Current State

The goblin tribe is now being led by the adventuring party now that Yek is gone. The Xanathar Guild's influence has diminished, and the Drow and his wererats have retreated deeper into the dungeon.
