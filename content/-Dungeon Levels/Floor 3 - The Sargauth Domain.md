# Major Events

### Initial State

The Legion of Azrok, led by the blind hobgoblin warleader Azrok and his wife Lurkana, was in conflict with the Drow family House Auvryndar. Azrok's magical dagger of truesight, which allowed him to lead his people, had been stolen by bandits who fled to Skullport.

### What did you do there?

The party met with Azrok and Lurkana, learned of the stolen dagger, and retrieved it from the bandits in Skullport. However, they were approached by a goblin controlled by a brain parasite of the Xanathar Guild, who offered them payment to weaken both sides of the conflict. The party warned the Drow of Azrok's plans, then led Azrok into battle, allowing him to sacrifice himself for his legion. They killed T'rissa Auvryndar, the leader of the Drow encampment, leaving the floor in chaos.

### Current State

The floor is now an empty husk. Most of its inhabitants are dead, and various delinquents from Skullport and the Xanathar Guild are seeking to settle here.
