**Description** | Halaster's Seven are the most trusted and powerful lieutenants of Halaster Blackcloak, the Mad Mage of Undermountain. Each member is a master of their magical discipline and serves as a teacher, enforcer, or agent of Halaster's will within the dungeon. Their presence shapes the politics, dangers, and mysteries of the Dweomercore and the greater Undermountain.
