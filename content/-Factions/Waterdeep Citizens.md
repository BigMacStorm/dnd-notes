# Waterdeep Citizens

A selection of notable and colorful members of Waterdeep society:

## Notable Citizens

- **Levi**  
  Brewer at 'Pig's Brewery'. Known for his hearty ales and friendly demeanor. A favorite among locals and adventurers alike.

- **Agatha**  
  Local old lady and skilled taxidermist. Agatha is a fixture in the community, famous for her stories and her lifelike animal displays.

- **Father Chris**  
  Priest and caretaker of the Waterdeep orphanage (now "Gertie's House"). Known for his compassion, dedication to the city's children, and willingness to work with adventurers for the greater good.

---
*This list represents just a few of the many unique individuals who make up the vibrant tapestry of Waterdeep's population.*
