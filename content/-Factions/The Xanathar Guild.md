**Description** | The Xanathar Guild is a group of cutthroats and bandits who run several of the top few floors of the Undermountain. Their main mission is to expand and claim territory within the Undermountain, using this influence to extract wealth and power. The guild is secretly led by the beholder [[Xanathar]], who is in league with a group of Illithids.
