**Leader |** [[Sundeth the Cruel]] (Public); [[Xanathar]] (Actual)

The Xanathar Guild is a group of cutthroats and bandits who run several of the top few floors of the Undermountain. The main mission of the Xanathar Guild is to expand and claim territory within the Undermountain, and then use this influence and power to wring money out of the dangerous situation. 

The Xanathar Guild is secretly being ran by the undead beholder [[Xanathar]], whom it is believed spends his days deep within the fortress of Skull Rock. The secret sect of the Xanathar Guild seems to be in league with a group of Illithids, who may or may not be causing a disturbance in Waterdeep, see the [[Investigate the reports of Illithids]] quest.
